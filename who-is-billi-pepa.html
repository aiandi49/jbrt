The Story

Keeper of a Vanished Legend

Billi Pepa is the guitarist and vocalist who founded Jimmibeetles Rock Theater — not as a band chasing a sound, but as a preservation project for a musician who chose to disappear rather than let his art be commercialized.

In 1996, Pepa released The Story of Jimmibeetles on the independent label No Records, produced by Zebrax and mastered by Pete Diorio at Sound Lab, NYC. The album collects both studio and live recordings, several pulled directly from Jimmibeetles' own 1960s and '70s tape archive.

"Jimmibeetles' profound essence and powerful soul swept the New York City rock scene during the 1960s as his audiences manifested, and his fans became fanatics. Ahead of his time, and overwrought with passion, Jimmibeetles undoubtedly influenced many of the superstar performers of his generation."

— Zebrax, former producer for Jimmibeetles, 1966–71

On the album, Pepa doesn't just play — he narrates. On the opening track, "Not From Here," his voice channels a "Circus Ring Master" character over a recording Jimmibeetles made live with Al Beads on zither at Ungano's, NYC, in 1968 — reportedly Robert Stigwood's favorite Jimmibeetles composition.

Beyond production, Pepa assembled a rotating cast of musicians to bring the songs to life on record — Tim Murphy and Michael Duff supplying what the liner notes call "the beat," and Eddie MaCoy and LaMar carrying "the spirit of theatre and performance" on bass and drums.

Nearly thirty years after that first release, Pepa is still the one steering the story — now working alongside the band's original drummer, LaMar, to bring the Jimmibeetles legend to a new generation.
