Press Release

History
is Made

New York, NY — 2026. The MetaEarth Music Hall of Fame announces its very first inductee: JBRT — JIMMIEBEETLES ROCK THEATER, the creative project behind New York hard rock ensemble The Wildstarfires. This is not merely a milestone — it is the moment an institution is born.

"The Wildstarfires is both an orphanage and a think tank of experienced Hard Rock musicians — focused on sound, on truth, on performance."

Formed in the crucible of New York City, The Wildstarfires captured decades of raw, visceral, live performance on tape — and that performance has never sounded more alive than it does right now. Best of The Wildstarfires Vol. I is Album No. 1 of 4 in a landmark collection being released through Bandcamp, giving fans worldwide direct access to purchase and stream this extraordinary catalog.

Three additional volumes — Best of The Wildstarfires Vol. II (June 12, 2024), Vol. III (October 31, 2024), and Best of The Wildstarfires Live (May 22, 2026) — complete one of the most ambitious archival releases in independent rock history. All four albums are available now on Bandcamp.
